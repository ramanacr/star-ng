# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want StarNG to be a friendly place for everyone. We pledge to make participation free from harassment, whatever your age, background, ability, identity, experience or appearance. We act and interact in ways that help an open, welcoming and healthy community. This text is a short version of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

## Acceptable behavior

- Be kind and patient with other people.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Give feedback that is clear and helpful, and accept feedback with grace.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the whole community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or images, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing the private information of others without their permission.
- Other conduct that people would reasonably call unprofessional.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and comments in this repository. It also applies when a person represents the project in public.

## How to report

1. To report a comment, use the "Report content" link on that comment. This tells GitHub and the maintainer.
2. If the report must stay private, use the [private vulnerability reporting page](https://github.com/ramanacr/star-ng/security/advisories/new). State that the report is about conduct, not about a vulnerability.

## What the maintainer does

The maintainer reads every report and keeps the reporter's identity private. The maintainer decides what action to take. Actions can be a private warning, a request to edit or remove a comment, a temporary block or a permanent block. The maintainer tells the people involved what was decided.
